Channel setup

Enter your Discord invitation links to connect these buttons to your existing Discord voice channels. These links are saved in this browser only. For a public deployment, edit the two link values in config.js so every visitor sees them.

Discord may first ask visitors to join your server, sign in, or select the voice channel.
