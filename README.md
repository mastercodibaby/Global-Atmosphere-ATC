📡 About Global Atmosphere

Global Atmosphere is an independent, browser-based voice communication project designed to provide an accessible environment for real-time communication without requiring users to create accounts or sign in.

Created by @mastercodibaby, Global Atmosphere explores a simple idea:

Communication should be accessible, straightforward, and independent of traditional social media platforms.

The project offers dedicated communication channels for everyday conversations and discussions concerning immediate threats, diplomacy, and the restoration of normalcy.

Global Atmosphere is intended to provide an alternative communication experience without relying on Discord accounts or conventional social networking systems.

✨ Key Features

- 🎙️ Voice Communication — Browser-based microphone controls for participating in voice channels.
- 🌍 Global Accessibility — Access the website from supported devices with an internet connection.
- 🔓 No Registration Required — Open the application without creating an account.
- 🛰️ Independent Platform — Designed as a standalone communication experience rather than a Discord integration.
- 📢 Dedicated Communication Channels — Choose a channel based on the purpose of your conversation.
- 📱 Cross-Device Access — Designed for browsers on smartphones, tablets, and computers.
- 🔇 Microphone Controls — Mute your microphone or leave a channel through the interface.
- 🌐 GitHub Pages Hosting — Publicly accessible through a static website.

📻 Communication Channels

◉ Channel 01 — Normalcy to Normalcy

Purpose: Everyday communication in normal, non-threatening situations.

This channel is intended for general conversations, friendly exchanges, discussions, and ordinary communication between participants.

◇ Channel 02 — Deplomacy to Normalcy

Purpose: Communication about immediate threats and restoring normalcy.

🚀 Getting Started

1. Visit the "Global Atmosphere website" (https://mastercodibaby.github.io/Global-Atmosphere-ATC/).
2. Select your preferred communication channel.
3. Click Join voice channel.
4. Allow microphone access when prompted by your browser.
5. Use the microphone controls to mute yourself when necessary.
6. Click Leave channel to exit.

No account, username, password, or registration is required to access the interface.

🎧 Microphone and Speaker Access

Global Atmosphere is designed around browser-based audio interaction.

For microphone access, users should:

- Use a modern browser with microphone support.
- Grant microphone permission when requested.
- Check their device's microphone and speaker settings.
- Maintain a stable internet connection.

Technical note: A microphone-enabled interface alone does not guarantee that audio is transmitted between participants. Actual multi-user voice communication requires a working real-time audio transport and signaling or relay infrastructure.

GitHub Pages hosts static website files; it does not independently provide a persistent voice server.

🌎 Our Mission

Global Atmosphere seeks to make communication more accessible through a simple, independent platform.

Its guiding objectives are:

Accessibility: Reduce barriers to entering communication spaces.

Independence: Provide a standalone alternative to conventional account-based communication services.

Simplicity: Make joining and leaving communication channels straightforward.

Peaceful Communication: Encourage responsible discussion, diplomacy, and the restoration of normalcy.

Global Reach: Make the application accessible to people across geographical boundaries wherever internet access and supported browsers are available.

🔐 Privacy and Responsibility

Global Atmosphere does not require application account registration to access its interface.

Users should nevertheless understand that:

- Browser permissions govern microphone access.
- Internet services may process connection metadata.
- Audio privacy depends on the underlying communication infrastructure.
- No account requirement does not automatically guarantee anonymity or end-to-end encryption.
- Users remain responsible for their communications and conduct.

Global Atmosphere should not be treated as a replacement for official emergency communication systems or professional air traffic control services.
